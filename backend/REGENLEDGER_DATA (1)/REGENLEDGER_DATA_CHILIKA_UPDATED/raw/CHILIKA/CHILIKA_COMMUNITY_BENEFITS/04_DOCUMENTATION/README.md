### Insulated Fish Boxes (IFB) – Post-harvest support
| Year | Boxes supplied | Beneficiaries |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| 2010-11 | 1,511 | — |
| 2011-12 | 1,226 | — |
| 2012-13 | 2,069 | — |
| **Total** | **4,806** | **4,806 traditional Chilika fishers** |

- Capacity: 70–100 litre  
- Subsidy: MPEDA 50% (₹50,93,450) + CDA 30% (₹30,56,070)  
- Outcome: maintained catch quality → **30–40% higher price realisation**  
- Status: **VERIFIED_HISTORICAL**

### Capacity building (NETFISH–CDA)
- 3,060 traditional fishers trained (2011-12 & 2012-13)  
- 102 training camps in **66 fishing villages** covering **71 PFCSs**

### Cooperative support
- 22 PFCSs received soft loan of ₹10 lakh each @ 4% simple interest (total ₹2.20 crore)
